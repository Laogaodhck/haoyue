Persist a piece of knowledge for future conversations: durable user preferences or corrections, project conventions, environment quirks, root causes of tricky bugs with their fixes, and decisions with their rationale. Use it when the user teaches something, corrects you, or when a hard-won finding would help future sessions. Titles act as stable keys — saving again with the same title updates that entry instead of duplicating it. Do not save one-off task details, anything already covered by MEMORY.md or AGENTS.md, or secrets (API keys, tokens, passwords).
